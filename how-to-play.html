Terje Medicine

SandShack runs Terje Medicine. Illness and injury move through stages, and the dose has to match the problem. A stronger medicine covers a weaker stage. Watch the condition instead of waiting until you are already down.

Terje Medicine wiki has the full item tables, timers, and overdose values. This is the field version.

How you take it

  • Tablets are for an early illness and for staying ahead of one. A pack holds ten, and packs of the same tablet combine.
  • Ampoules are the strong dose once a sickness has taken hold. You need a syringe. After the shot it is dirty: disinfect it with alcohol, or boil it in a pot over a fire, before you use it again.
  • Injectors are that same kind of dose in a one-use device. No syringe.

Clean hands, clean tools

Disinfect your hands and any medical gear before you work on a wound. Alcohol tincture and disinfectant spray are the kit for that. Dirty hands on an open wound are how a cut becomes sepsis. After surgery, keep the sutures clean the same way.

Vaccines

Take a vaccine before you are sick. It cuts the chance of that disease and it wears off, so you take it again later. Vaxicam covers a cold, Zerivax the zombie virus, and Rabivax rabies.

Wounds

  • A stab or a bullet needs a bandage immediately. A hemostatic bandage beats rags. The wound can keep weeping until surgery takes the bullet or the blade out. A field surgical kit, an IFAK, or an AFAK does that job. Organ damage is the same work, only harder.
  • A vest can stop the round and still leave a bruise. Bruises from zombies and blunt hits pile up and start costing health. They fade on their own. Viprosal, Capsicum, or Finalgon speeds that up.
  • A hard hit to the head, a nearby blast, or a round caught by a helmet can concuss you: headache, dizziness, bad balance, blurred sight. It fades. Noopept or Neirox shortens it.
  • Vikasol tablets and the Erytromixelin injector slow bleeding so you have time to bandage. Irovit, magnesium sulfate, and erythropoietin rebuild blood after you have already lost it.

Illness

  • Cold comes from staying cold. Rest somewhere warm and a mild cold can pass. Leave it and it becomes pneumonia, and your health starts to drop. Use an antibiotic at least as strong as the stage you are in, and expect more than one dose.
  • Bad food, rotten fruit, the wrong mushroom, dirty water, or a mouthful of gasoline is food poisoning. Nausea hits fast, then dehydration. Drink water. Charcoal is the light dose. Stronger antipoison meds if it is already bad.
  • Toxic gas is chemical poisoning: a cough, then vomiting, then it can kill you. Take an antidote, such as the AntiChem injector.
  • The zombie virus travels in the air and in fluids. A fight is a small chance. A wound from the infected is a much bigger one. A mask and armor lower the odds. You have a long window, more than an hour, to find Zivirol before the last stage crashes your health.
  • Sepsis is an infected open wound. Health slips away until you use a strong antibiotic.
  • Rabies comes from bites, wolves included. It starts quiet: a temperature and a weakness you can miss. Next you cannot keep food or water down. Then it kills you. Treat it early, or take Rabivax before you are bitten.

Pain and too many pills

Pain eases by itself. A painkiller of the right strength, or stronger, cuts it short. Medicines also fill an overdose meter. Stage one is nausea and hallucinations. Stage two can knock you out. Stage three threatens your life. It fades as the drugs leave your system. Water is the light treatment for the first stage. Do not take a second dose because the first one felt slow.

Sleep

You have to sleep, the same way you have to eat and drink. Yellow on the sleep meter means yawning and fading sight. Red means you can pass out. Sleep warm: a fire, a house, a tent, or a sleeping bag. You cannot sleep if you are freezing, starving, or carrying a stage-two illness. Sleep restores health and blood, and a light cold can break while you rest. Energy drinks buy the meter back when you cannot lie down.

Mind

Zombie attacks, psi zones, and human meat wear the mind down into panic and hallucinations. Antidepressants steady it. A psi zone is worse the closer you stand to the middle. If the mind hits the bottom, the character can do something fatal. A PSI helmet is the gear built for those zones. Take an antidepressant before you walk in.

Base Building Plus

Most pieces start as a frame. Wood or planks go on that frame. Metal sheet needs the wood tier already finished. Concrete is bricks and a mortar mix, and you have to be holding a shovel or a pickaxe. The small half-wall is the exception: one concrete brick and the tool, no mortar.
